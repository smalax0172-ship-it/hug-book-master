# Downloading Blake Plates from the Blake Archive

Use this procedure to download the complete gallery for **Songs of Innocence and of Experience, copy B**, rather than a single plate.

## Source

- [Songs of Innocence and of Experience, copy B](https://www.blakearchive.org/copy/songsie.b)

## Download the Complete Copy

1. Install a page-image grabber for your browser:
   - **Chrome:** Imageye
   - **Firefox:** DownThemAll
2. Open the copy link above and confirm that the plates appear as a gallery.
3. Scroll all the way to the bottom of the gallery **before opening the extension**. The gallery loads images as you scroll, so the grabber cannot capture images that have not yet appeared on screen.
4. Open the image-grabber extension. Sort or filter the results by image size, select the large plate images, and deselect icons and thumbnails.
5. Download the selected images into a folder named for the copy, such as `songsie-copy-b`, so plates from other copies remain separate.

## Verify the Download

Open the destination folder after the grabber finishes and confirm that:

- the folder contains the full sequence of plates shown in the gallery;
- the files are plate images, not interface icons or thumbnails;
- several files open at a large, readable resolution; and
- no browser-download errors remain.

Do not treat opening the gallery or selecting images in the extension as a completed download. The files must be present in the copy-specific destination folder before this procedure is finished.

## If the Downloaded Images Are Fuzzy or Small

The grabber may have downloaded the gallery's preview layer instead of the full-resolution files. For each affected plate:

1. Open the plate.
2. Open its full-size or enlargement view.
3. Right-click the enlarged image and save it directly.

This fallback is slower, but it retrieves the full-resolution image instead of the preview.

## Use Restriction

Use the downloaded scans only for study and personal reading under the Blake Archive's conditions of use. Do not use them in anything published, distributed commercially, or sold without obtaining the necessary permission.
